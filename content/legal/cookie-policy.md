This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are and how we use them on **HeimaEden** (`https://heimaeden.com`). You should read this policy so you can understand what type of cookies we use, the information we collect using cookies, and how that information is used.

## What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files that are used to store small pieces of information. They are stored on your device when the website is loaded on your browser. These cookies help us make the website function properly, make it more secure, provide a better user experience, and analyze how the website performs.

## How Do We Use Cookies?
HeimaEden uses first-party and third-party cookies for several purposes:
* **Necessary Cookies**: These cookies are essential for you to experience the full functionality of our site (e.g., maintaining user sessions).
* **Analytics Cookies**: We use third-party tools like Google Analytics to track and measure usage of this site so that we can continue to produce engaging technical content.
* **Advertising Cookies**: Google AdSense and our ad partners use these cookies to serve advertisements that are relevant to your technical interests and to track ad performance.

## How Can You Control Cookies?
You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the cookie consent banner on our homepage. Additionally, most browsers allow you to refuse or accept cookies through their built-in settings.
